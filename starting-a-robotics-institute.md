# How to Start an Educational Robotics Institute

Starting a robotics institute requires planning, suitable equipment, and a clear understanding of local education needs.

## Steps to Get Started

1. Research the demand for robotics education in your location.
2. Select a suitable franchise model.
3. Understand the total investment and operational expenses.
4. Arrange robotics kits, computers, and other equipment.
5. Prepare a suitable learning space.
6. Recruit or train instructors.
7. Plan student courses and practical workshops.
8. Promote your institute among schools and students.

Before investing, carefully review the franchise agreement and available support services.
