# Low-Investment Robotics Franchise: Important Considerations

Entrepreneurs exploring a low-investment educational robotics franchise should evaluate all costs before starting.

## Important Cost Factors

- Franchise or licensing fees
- Robotics kits and equipment
- Computers and software
- Classroom setup
- Instructor salaries
- Marketing and promotion
- Rent and operational expenses

## How to Evaluate a Franchise

Compare different franchise models and check what is included in the initial investment. Ask about training, curriculum, technical assistance, equipment, and ongoing fees.

Avoid choosing a franchise based only on low starting costs. Review the complete business model before making a decision.
