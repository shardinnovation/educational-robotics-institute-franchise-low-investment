# Benefits of an Educational Robotics Franchise

An educational robotics franchise combines technology learning with business opportunities.

## Major Benefits

1. **STEM Education:** Introduce students to science, technology, engineering, and mathematics through practical activities.
2. **Robotics Skills:** Help students understand sensors, motors, circuits, and programming.
3. **AI and Coding:** Offer learning opportunities in artificial intelligence and computer programming.
4. **Practical Projects:** Encourage creativity and problem-solving through hands-on activities.
5. **Structured Learning:** Use organised course materials and project-based teaching methods.

A well-planned robotics education centre can support students in developing useful technology skills.
